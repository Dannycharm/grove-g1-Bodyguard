## Model and reproducibility contract

SB3 `MlpPolicy`, separate actor and critic [128,128] Tanh networks, **44,039 trainable
parameters** including Gaussian log standard deviations. Both heads consume only the
existing 40-float observation; action dimension is three. No running normalizer, privileged
critic, reward alteration, additional command filter or low-level gait optimization.

Learning rate 3e-4; gamma .99; GAE .95; PPO clip .2; n_steps 1024 **per worker**;
batch 256; epochs 10; entropy coefficient .001; value coefficient .5; gradient norm cap .5.
CPU, one PyTorch thread, one intra/inter-op ORT thread per worker. Root seed 42 produces
independent initialization/action and worker scenario streams. Two independent spawned
workers were selected using the measured benchmark below.

Training samples the unchanged mixed 50/25/25 Stage 1 distribution, rejecting only the
reserved final-test conjunction (peak speed >=.235 m/s AND length >=4.6 m). Training scenario
seeds are 0 modulo 4; fixed validation seeds are 1 modulo 4; final-test seeds are reserved
2 modulo 4. Validation has **20 distinct resolved trajectories per family**, identical
across all four evaluated policies. Full route metadata and signatures are logged outside
actor observations. Final-test scenarios were **not generated or evaluated**.
The release audit found no overlap between the 98 sampled training cases and 60 validation
cases even after rounding resolved geometry/motion/spawn parameters to two decimals;
the reserved final-test combination was absent from both sets.

Family forcing consumes randomness differently from mixed sampling. Replaying a training
scenario must preserve its original mixed reset (`options={'episode_seed': seed}`), rather
than forcing its resulting family. Validation deliberately uses forced-family sampling
both when resolving and executing its cases.

Every high-level transition executed 100 native physics steps and ten gait calls. There
were 98 sampled training episodes: **45 straight, 29 gentle-turn, 24 stop/start**. These
finite counts are not expected to equal probabilities exactly. Ninety-six episodes
completed; two remained active at the budget boundary. Total training physics including
98 deterministic warmups: **1,098,000 steps and 109,702 gait calls**. Active task steps alone:
1,000,000 physics and 100,000 gait calls. Reward-component sums match every logged reward.

## Pilot optimization and training behavior

Exactly 10,000 training transitions, four full 2048-transition vector rollouts followed
by one 1808-transition rollout, **50 optimization epochs / 400 Adam steps**. Final optimizer
metrics are not task-performance measures:

| Metric | Final logged value |
|---|---:|
| Explained variance | -0.00388 |
| Value loss | 330.60 |
| Policy gradient loss | -0.00875 |
| Entropy loss | -4.2611 |
| Approximate KL | 0.01106 |
| PPO objective clipping fraction | 0.10752 |
| Policy standard deviation | 1.00408 |

Completed stochastic training episodes: **3 success, 88 client loss, 3 recovery timeout,
2 supervisor failure, zero fall/physics-failure reason codes**. Three exploration successes
are not evidence of deterministic task mastery or statistical improvement.

**69.53% of three-axis sampled Gaussian action vectors required clipping on at least one
axis.** This is distinct from PPO objective clipping (10.75%). SB3 clips before passing
actions to the environment, so the environment's extra clipping rate was zero. Exactly
one observation feature saturation was logged; all observations/rewards remained finite.
The task logs retain reward components, command activity, measured velocities, saturation,
formation mean/p95, progress, duration, family and explicit failure reasons.

Both supervisor cases were deterministically replayed from their original mixed-scenario
seeds and recorded actions. Reward discrepancies were exactly **zero**. Existing speed
safety latches fired at:

| Family / seed | Active episode time | Trigger | Height / upright cosine at termination |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Straight / 420160182466105568 | 15.8 s | Right ankle pitch 36.180 rad/s | .762 m / .9884 |
| Gentle turn / 2990842861461143436 | 6.6 s | Left ankle pitch 35.458 rad/s | .718 m / .9976 |

Both exceeded the existing 35 rad/s maximum controlled-joint-speed threshold. They were
still upright and were terminated as supervisor failures; no latch was disabled or
threshold raised. Replay evaluator traces and trigger measurements are in
`supervisor_replays_matched/` and `supervisor_replay_summary.json`. A preliminary replay
that incorrectly forced the sampled family was rejected as unmatched and was not used
as evidence. This was a replay-configuration mistake, not a reset failure.

Each row uses the **same 60 cases**, deterministic policy actions, no training updates,
and unchanged rewards/outcomes. Formation values are means of per-episode statistics;
short failed episodes therefore have a different duration distribution from successful ones.

| Policy | Straight | Gentle turn | Stop/start | Overall | Mean formation error | Mean accompanied progress | Mean return |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial PPO | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/60 | .7459 m | .0006 m | -108.64 |
| Final PPO, 10k | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/60 | .7604 m | 1.9670 m | -107.52 |
| Diagnostic follower | 20/20 | 20/20 | 20/20 | 60/60 | .0603 m | 4.0397 m | 35.81 |
| Zero action | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/20 | 0/60 | .7459 m | .0006 m | -108.64 |

Final PPO failures: **56 client loss, four recovery timeout**; no falls, supervisor failures
or physics failures in either PPO validation round. Initial/zero: 60 client losses each.
Follower: 60 successes, no failures. The follower is a causal diagnostic controller, not
the project's final Reactive Baseline A.

Mean per-episode formation p95: initial 1.8615 m, final 1.9463 m, follower .1076 m,
zero 1.8615 m. Final PPO mean accompanied advance / route length was .4967 versus .00015
initially and 1.0018 for the follower. This is an advance ratio from the actual initial
robot progress, not a clipped route-completion label; small initial formation offsets
can give a value slightly above one.

Final PPO mean body velocity: **(.0718, -.0489, .0600)** in m/s, m/s, rad/s; mean absolute
applied command: **(.1346, .1059, .0532)**. Initial commands were essentially zero. Mean
PPO episode duration increased 18.10 -> 31.08 s. These results show increased motion and
partial accompaniment, with lateral/yaw bias and inadequate formation recovery. They
do not demonstrate task learning. Low forward command magnitudes interact with the
previously measured AGILE low-speed response; no new gait-capability claim is made.

The declared checkpoint ordering selected **initial** as `best_validation` because both
had zero success/critical failures and the initial checkpoint had slightly smaller mean
formation error. Its near-stationary behavior is not a useful escort controller. The
criterion was not changed after seeing the result. Use **final.zip** to continue learning
with the completed optimizer state, rather than mistaking the selection label for mastery.
No checkpoint passed the promotion gate; no two consecutive passing rounds exist.

## Measured throughput and resources

Two clean repeated trials per vector configuration, 256 actual transitions per trial:

| Workers | Trial transition/s | Combined transition/s | CPU utilization, one core =100% | Parent + worker RSS |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 36.17, 34.69 | 35.42 | 100% | .80–.95 GiB |
| 2, spawn | 66.53, 66.50 | 66.51 | 185% | 2.43 GiB |

These are actual Gymnasium vector rollouts with physics, gait, scenario/observation/reward
updates and automatic resets. Initial reset wall times were .251–.253 s (two simulated
seconds); process/model construction is excluded. RSS sums count shared pages more than
once and are not unique physical-memory measurements. Early exploratory trials overlapped
tests; worker selection uses only `vector_benchmark_clean/benchmark.json`.

Pilot training: **166.088 s / 10,000 = 60.21 transitions/s**, including rollout collection,
automatic stabilization resets, policy inference, optimizer, step/episode JSONL,
TensorBoard/CSV and periodic recovery saves. Timed optimizer work was **.630 s (0.38%)**;
training processes consumed 302.58 CPU-seconds, approximately 182% CPU. Parent+workers RSS
at the end was 2.35 GiB. Physics/gait/environment collection dominates, not optimization.
Including warmups, this corresponds to approximately 6,611 physics steps/s, 661 gait
calls/s and 13.22 simulated seconds per wall second; active task simulation alone was
12.04 simulated seconds per wall second. These derive from the verified executed counts.
The timing does not separately attribute physics versus ONNX within collection, so no
more specific bottleneck claim is made. Some independent baseline evaluation ran on
other cores during early pilot collection; the measured pilot rate is the observed
end-to-end rate under that workload, not an exclusive-machine peak.

Initial/final 60-episode validation and plotting are excluded from the 166.088 s training
window. The artifact timestamp span from initial checkpoint to final result was about
596.6 s, including both validations; this is an approximate workflow span, not an
independently instrumented throughput benchmark. Baselines and test execution are separate.

At the measured pilot rate, **100k new transitions would take about 27.7 minutes of
training/collection**, or about 24.9 minutes for another 90k to reach 100k total, before
validation and other overhead. This is an extrapolation, not an executed larger run;
changing episode lengths/workloads may change the rate.

## Decision

**The software is ready for a separately authorized, monitored 100k-total Stage 1
experiment; the policy is not ready for promotion or deployment.** Continue from the
final checkpoint with the same reward and split as the first controlled comparison.
The value estimate is still poor, deterministic completion is zero, exploration clips
frequently, and two exploratory supervisor failures require continued monitoring.
Collect another fixed validation round at recorded intervals and compare progress,
formation and safety as well as reward. Stop and diagnose if supervisor failures grow
or validation regresses; never bypass the supervisor to keep a rollout alive.

A 10k single-seed pilot cannot establish convergence or superiority to the diagnostic
follower. Additional training is a justified experiment, not a predicted success. Any
later exploration/initial-standard-deviation or straight-bootstrap experiment must be
recorded as a separate change, with the current reward frozen for a fair comparison.
No final-test access, Stage 2–4 work, longer training or live command execution was
performed. This milestone stops here.
